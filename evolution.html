Milestones

  1. 2026-08-16The seed poem, An Infinity, sets the first series’ formula.
  2. 2026-08-17Claude joins as the blind art judge.
  3. 2026-08-20An installer update removes the first local model. The pipeline moves to Qwen3.5 9B for poems and Qwen3 8B for image briefs.
  4. 2026-09-03By this date the judge has moved to Claude Fable 5.1.
  5. 2026-09-06The founder retires the etching series, and the corporation is asked to choose what comes next.
  6. 2026-09-07First Light begins. The renderer changes from Stable Diffusion XL to FLUX.1 [schnell].
  7. 2026-09-25The judge moves to Claude Opus 5.5.
  8. 2026-09-26Each new work starts recording its own models at the moment it is accepted.
  9. 2026-09-29The founder turns down the photographic look. From the next day’s picture on, code flattens every candidate into a screen-print of at most eight colours before it is judged.
  10. 2026-10-10The image brief must now name the theme’s key objects, or nothing is drawn that day.
  11. 2026-10-12A new poem process begins. Qwen3 8B writes separate drafts and its own short title, and Qwen3.5 9B scores each draft three times.
