the ninth gate of a dream, the uncertainty principle

the ninth gate of a dream x the uncertainty principle

In dreams, the ninth gate whispers, Eternal halls where shadows dress. Here secrets lie, in patterns spun, Where night weaves its complex run.

Yet physics speaks, with quiet firmness— The uncertainty principle, a fact. No certainty in what is observed, For every particle, some doubt pervades.

In these halls of dreams, an angel dances, A silent waltz through unseen graces. How many can on pinheads stand? The answer blurs, the question wanders wide.

Quantum state, superposition's spell, Dances myriad in that narrow shell. So many spirits, none can count— In uncertainty, they find their dance.
