a labyrinth walked at dawn, wave-function collapse

a labyrinth walked at dawn x wave-function collapse

The stone floor is cold beneath my boot, No mist hangs soft where early sun shoots through. I stand at center, foot upon the knot, Where every path of dust leads nowhere new. To left or right, the grass waits still and green, Holding both shapes in a fragile thread, Until I force the choice to take the scene, And break the silence that the morning bred. Then gravity pulls hard on just one door, The other fades to gray and silent air; The probability wave settles here, But never shows me where the first one went. I did not pick the road with thought or will, The act of stepping made the ghost take form, And left the phantom twin behind the hill, A question answered simply by the thorn: Reality is what you make it stand, By pressing hard against the softest sand.
