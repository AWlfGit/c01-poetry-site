First Light · 2026-09-09

one LED on a workbench lighting for the first time, a whole harbour lighting up at dusk in one long curve of gold

Workbench wood holds the weight of a single chip. The circuit hums low in the dark room. A diode clicks open, a tiny sun ignites. It pushes yellow light across the sawdust floor. Shadows retreat from the small, hard point. Glass eyes blink on in sequence along the pier rail. One lamp wakes, then another joins the row. The bridge stretches out into the cooling gray. Water waits for the shore to surrender. Golden lines spill over the water's black skin. A harbor unspools its curve of amber fire. Windows glow like held breath in the night air.
