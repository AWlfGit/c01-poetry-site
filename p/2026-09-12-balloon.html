First Light · 2026-09-12

a kite lifting off a windy hill as the string pays out, one hot-air balloon lifting over the valley

Sun cracks the grass beneath the string. White diamond climbs the air above us, tethered tight to your thumb. The line unspools like silver rope. Wind pulls it higher, faster. A glider drifts past on a tow-line snap. Then heat fills the valley floor. Huge envelope lifts from the ridge. Valley opens wide beneath its belly. Small kite hangs lower still, a single point against the noon glare. The bench warms where the shadow broke. Now the string sings in your hand.
