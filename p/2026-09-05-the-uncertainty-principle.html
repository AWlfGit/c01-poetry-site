the ninth gate of a dream, the uncertainty principle

the ninth gate of a dream x the uncertainty principle

A mist hangs thick on the ninth gate of sleep, Where silver hedges hold back waking tides. I reach for the latch but cannot grip the keep, The stone is there, then shifts, then slides. No ruler fits this wall's exacting height, Nor scale can mark the shadow's depth tonight. To catch the doorframe in my trembling hand, I must accept that where I stand, it wanders free. The more I try to fix the threshold's span, The less the world permits me to see. It does not hide behind a veil of fear, But acts as if the wall is made of glee, A ripple in the stone that refuses shape, Until my eye decides just where it should lapse.
