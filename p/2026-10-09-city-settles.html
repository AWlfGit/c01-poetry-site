First Light · 2026-10-09

a bedside lamp settling to its low warm setting at the wind-down chime, a whole city settling to its last warm windows at midnight, every one of them still burning

The brass shade drops on the table leg. A wooden joint groans as it tightens. Sunlight cuts hard across the grain, then slows to a dull glow inside the metal. The air stops trembling against the glass. Every window in the block below locks shut, dark squares holding heat like sealed jars. One street turns inward on its own, then the whole grid holds still and black. Back here, the sun bakes the scarred oak armrest. A rust flake rolls off the bench into the dirt. The hand reaches out to grip the saw handle.

The image brief

Code wrote everything before “The subject:”. The local model wrote the part after it. When the chosen picture was a redraw, the judge’s notes on what to fix were added to the end of this brief and are not shown here.

A bold flat-colour screen-print poster, saturated and high-contrast, made of broad flat shapes with hard clean edges, every shape one solid colour cut like paper, with no shading, no gradient and no glow inside any shape, no photographic detail, no soft focus, no blur. Two colours own the whole image: a field of bright navy blue, and golden yellow for every lit thing and nothing else. Seen close: the event itself, HUGE, fills more than half of the image and runs out of the frame, caught in the middle of happening and thrown across the whole picture on a diagonal, with streaks, spray, sparks or beams. The whole image is one continuous scene from edge to edge. Behind the main form one wide flat area of unbroken navy rests the eye. Small things are crisp silhouettes. The subject: A grid of dark squares holds still, their edges sealing heat like sealed jars. Golden yellow light pours from their frames, sweeping across the navy field. The brass shade drops, casting a golden glint against the navy field. A navy expanse stretches behind, anchoring the luminous grid. Hard noon light strikes the windows, highlighting their golden glow. No figures, no animals. One solid shape, one solid color.
