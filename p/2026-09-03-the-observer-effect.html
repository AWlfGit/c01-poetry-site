the alchemist's crucible x the observer effect

The copper bowl sits waiting in the dark, To turn the lead within to golden spark. A single grain falls from the velvet air, And changes all the weight that hangs inside. I lift my gaze to watch the fire rise, And suddenly the vapor clears my eyes. Before I looked, the mercury was both, Liquid and gas, a blur of heat and cloth. The moment sight touches the boiling fluid, It snaps to steam or stays as liquid food. No hidden truth exists without the eye, To force the outcome from a deeper sky. So ask not how much gold is in the mix, But what your watching makes the chaos fix.
