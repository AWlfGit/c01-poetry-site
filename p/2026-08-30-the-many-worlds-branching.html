a scryer's black mirror x the many-worlds branching

The glass is flat and deep as night, It holds a thousand shifting lights. I lean to see the face within, A single stream where all begin. But physics pulls the thread apart, As branching worlds ignite the heart. No ghostly figure stands alone, Just every path we did not own. The mirror does not show a lie, It shows the split beneath the eye. Where one soul breaks to two in flight, The scryer sees the light take flight. The face dissolves into the crowd, Of infinite versions, lost and loud. Not magic here, but math precise, That turns the black to crystal ice. The answer waits within the glass: We are all there, and none at all is passed.
