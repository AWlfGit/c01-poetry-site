a labyrinth walked at dawn, wave-function collapse

a labyrinth walked at dawn x wave-function collapse

In mists at dawn, the labyrinth unwinds, A path through shadows, where light threads align. Each step a quest in ancient, quiet verse, Where whispers of the first to speak their curse.

Across this maze, my feet tread without sound, Yet physics' laws demand a different ground. Wave-function collapses, states abruptly bind, Particles in superposition dance and spin.

In this quantum realm, not only light but shade, Entangled pairs, entwined in space-wide range. Like photons flickering, here and gone in turn, Here angels whisper: "Dance, then disappear, return."

Walls twist and turn, yet each a portal too, A paradox of paths where many meet and pursue. The labyrinth speaks in ancient mystic’s tongue, Yet physics answers with the whispering sun.
