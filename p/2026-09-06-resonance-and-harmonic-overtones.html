votive candles in a row, resonance and harmonic overtones

votive candles in a row x resonance and harmonic overtones

Small wax heads burn down to matchsticks tight, Arranged in straight and silent stone-light rows. Each flame sends up a wavering, white-hot light That bounces soft against the glassy show. One flicker starts a vibration in the air, A pressure wave that spreads from wick to wick. The second flame begins to hear its pair And locks into the rhythm of the trick. They do not burn as separate, isolated sparks, But swell together where the standing waves align. The quiet air holds tension and dark arcs Of sound that makes the shadows dance in time. It was not magic making them glow bright, Nor spirits dancing on the pin's wide head; Just physics holding fast to steady light, Where infinite tones are born inside of bread
