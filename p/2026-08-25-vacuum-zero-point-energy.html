a pilgrim counting a rosary, vacuum zero-point energy

a pilgrim counting a rosary x vacuum zero-point energy

Small beads move through a heavy hand's slow turn, While empty space hums a vibration sharp and pure, A field that fills the dark beneath every fern, A restless sea of potential, vast and obscure. The monk counts fifty prayers to calm the mind, But atoms jitter where he sits alone, Where silence hides a density so blind That zero holds more noise than stone or bone. He asks if God exists above the wire, She answers with the heat that shakes the glass, No angels dance in such a tiny fire, Just probability spread out in mass. Ten thousand billion pairs of phantom light Live inside the prayer before it starts to be, A constant hum where nothing has any weight, Making his holy count seem quiet and weak.
